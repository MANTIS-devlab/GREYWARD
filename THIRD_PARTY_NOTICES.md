## ArtCraft office applications

GREYWARD Writer, Sheets, PDF and Slides are downstream integrations of
ArtCraft's [WordCraft](https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft),
[GridCraft](https://github.com/storytold/gridcraft),
[PdfCraft](https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft) and
[DeckCraft](https://github.com/storytold/deckcraft), respectively. Their upstream
authors and contributors created the application engines and interfaces;
GREYWARD maintains its packaging and desktop/visual adaptations. These are
customized GREYWARD builds, not official ArtCraft releases.

Original licenses, copyright notices and resource attribution are preserved in
the packages. See the [integration and provenance records](docs/software/OFFICE_INTEGRATION.md)
and [resource-license notes](packaging/greyward-office/licenses/README.md) for the
pinned sources and retained legal material.
